Our Story

Rooted in the PNW.
Driven by Family.

For decades, the names Lee Johnson and Dwayne Lane have stood for more than just car dealerships — they have represented a commitment to the communities of Western Washington. Today, we are proud to announce the formal union of these two legacies into the Lane-Johnson Auto Family.

By combining our resources, we aren't just growing our inventory — we're expanding our ability to serve you. From the first turn of the key to the final service milestone, our mission remains the same: treating every customer like a member of our own family. Different names, different locations, but one shared standard of integrity.
