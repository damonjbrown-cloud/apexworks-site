The Stylist

Wesley
Brian

A hairstylist since 1999, Wesley trained at Gene Juarez Hair Academy — completing both the full program and the instructor track. His career expanded across Seattle-area salons, into education with Paul Mitchell, and onto the stage at hair shows across Washington and Oregon.

In 2004, he opened Wesley Brian Salon in Monroe, WA. His specialty: precision and dry cutting, using texture to build customized styles and dimensional color that moves with you.
