What we do

Website audits, Google search, and AI answers

Most local sites are hard to judge from the inside. You know the business. You do not have to know why Google skipped the page, why the map listing looks empty, or why a customer who asked an AI tool never heard your name. That is the work.

We keep the language plain. A website audit is a written look at the site and the Google presence, with the fixes ranked by what is most likely to bring in calls. It is not a stack of scores with no next step.

Website audits

We read the site the way a customer would, then the way Google would. You get a plain-English score of the website and of your Google presence, plus the short list of fixes that matter first.

Google search

We look at how you show up when someone nearby searches for what you sell. That includes your pages, your titles, and your Google Business Profile. The goal is more calls, not a prettier report.

AI optimization

People now ask ChatGPT and Google's AI answers for a roofer, a clinic, or a shop. We check whether those tools can find you and recommend you, and we say what to change if they cannot.
